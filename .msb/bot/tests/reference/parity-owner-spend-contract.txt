Saved-action implementation of the owner's new policy (local only): a due
literal reminder or saved prompt observed while costPaused stores one
costPausePending occurrence under the existing action-file lock. The marker
retains the original trigger, schedule revision and previous attempt. A later
scheduler pass accepts that occurrence even outside its cron minute. Multiple
missed slots coalesce; no attempt or run journal is written while deferred.
The old cost-pause notice is sent once; a changed quota/budget refusal is
reported once and retains the pending action until the separate limit allows it.
Unknown marker versions are preserved; a changed schedule, newer attempt,
deleted row or disabled task cannot launch the stale occurrence. Text edits and
a user's off/on choice retain the applicable occurrence without reverting edits.

After claim, the literal path rechecks expense and owner pause before sending;
saved prompts propagate a detected prelaunch pause through stop, preparation
failure and early model/freshness error. A state-read failure at that final
fence also defers the unstarted occurrence. A quick resume after a paused
preflight must first save the due slot and retry all limits on the next tick;
it cannot skip the box-budget read. /stop epoch changes prevent a not-yet-sent
literal during the added asynchronous check. These are admission checks, not
an atomic transaction with a real external service.
