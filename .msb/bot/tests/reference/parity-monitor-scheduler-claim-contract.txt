The scheduler takes a non-blocking SQLite claim for (monitor directory,
sanitized botId) before the whole scheduled poll and digest; a busy claim skips
that tick. The claim is separate from the config, items and meta locks and is
released by the OS on process exit. Different bot IDs remain independent.
The explicit manual pollAllSources route does not take this claim. If stop()
occurs while claim acquisition is pending, the admitted callback checks stopped
before starting any fetch. A claim error is logged and does not fall through to
unclaimed collection. The claim creates a per-bot lock database under
monitor/.scheduler-claims; it is not deleted automatically on rollback.

This only coordinates scheduled ticks using this claim. Manual collection or an
external direct call to pollAllSources({scheduled:true}) can overlap; a crash
after a remote fetch or message but before metadata is saved can cause a retry.
The claim protects normal symlink aliases tested on macOS and Linux, not a
separate hardlinked meta.json, directory replacement during a tick or unrelated
non-cooperating writers. A long-running or hung tick holds the claim: another
instance skips its tick, and a digest may be delayed or miss its configured
hour. stop() after work starts does not cancel the active poll. These are limits,
not a claim of exactly-once execution or guaranteed deadline delivery. Revert
the behavior commit to restore prior admission; retain or inspect lock files
